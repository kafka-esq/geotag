Privacy policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

What we collect

  • Your tag name and who invited you, so the crew system works.
  • What you post: the paint data of your pieces, your comments, and props you give.
  • Where walls are: when you post a piece, the location and orientation of that wall is saved so others can find it. Your own location is used on your phone to find nearby walls and is sent to our server only as part of looking up walls near you. We don't keep a history of where you've been.
  • Reports and blocks you make, so moderation works.

We don't collect your name, email, phone number or contacts. Sign-in is an anonymous account stored in your phone's keychain.

Camera

The camera is used live to find walls and show art. We don't upload or store photos or video. When you use sketch mode, a still of the wall is used on your phone only.

Google ARCore

geo# uses Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), provided by Google LLC. To work out precisely where your phone is, ARCore sends visual data from the camera to Google, which matches it against Street View imagery. When a wall is "fingerprinted" for better accuracy, ARCore uploads visual feature data of that spot to Google. This is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and the ARCore privacy requirements.

Where it's stored

Our data is stored with Supabase (database hosting). We don't sell data and we don't use it for advertising or tracking.

Deleting your data

Delete your account from the "you" screen in the app. That removes your profile, pieces, comments, props, reports and blocks. Walls stay on the map only while other people's art is on them.

Children

geo# is not for children under 13.

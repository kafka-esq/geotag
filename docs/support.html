Common problems

The status never turns green.
geo# matches what your camera sees against Street View. Stand across the street from buildings and point at them, not at the ground or sky. It doesn't work indoors or where Street View hasn't been.
"Lock on" stays grey.
The crosshair needs a big flat wall, 1 to 6 metres away. Move the phone slowly from side to side so it can measure the wall. Glass, cars, trees and small surfaces are skipped on purpose.
My crew code doesn't work.
Each code works once. Ask whoever invited you for another, or ask them to check their codes on the "you" screen.
I want to delete my account.
Open the "you" screen and tap "delete my account" at the bottom.

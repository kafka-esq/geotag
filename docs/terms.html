Terms of use and crew rules

Last updated October 29, 2026

By using geo# you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, don't use the app.

Zero tolerance

geo# has no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. We remove content and accounts that break these rules, and we act on reports within 24 hours. Because geo# is invite only, the person who invited a rule-breaker may lose access too.

Crew rules

  • No hate speech, slurs, threats, harassment or bullying.
  • No sexual content, nudity or gore.
  • No names, addresses, phone numbers, faces or other personal details of real people without their permission.
  • No spam, scams or advertising.
  • Going over someone's piece is part of the game. Covering pieces only to wreck them, or targeting one person, is harassment.
  • geo# is digital. Don't paint, damage or trespass on real property, and don't use geo# to encourage it.
  • Pay attention to where you are. Don't walk into traffic or private property while using the app.

Your content

You own what you paint. You give geo# permission to store it and show it to other members at the wall where you painted it, in the app's map and activity views, and in moderation tools. Deleting a piece or your account removes it.

Reporting and blocking

Every piece, comment and person in the app has a report option. Blocking someone hides their art and comments from you and yours from them. Reported content is hidden automatically once several members report it, and reviewed by a person.

Accounts

You can delete your account at any time from the "you" screen. That removes your profile, pieces, comments and props.

No warranty

geo# is a test version provided as is. Positions of art can be off, and things may break or be reset.

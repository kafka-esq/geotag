geo#

Every wall in the city is a canvas. Only your crew can see it.

geo# is an augmented reality app for iPhone. Your phone becomes a spray can: aim the camera at a real wall, hold, and move. The paint stays on that wall, so the next person who points their phone at it sees your piece, gives it props, comments, or goes over it.

It's invite only. If someone gave you a crew code, open the TestFlight link they sent you, install geo#, and enter the code when it asks.

No code? You can still try practice mode inside the app. Nothing gets saved or shared.

What's different about it

  • Paint can physics: distance sets the spray width, angle stretches it, lingering makes it drip, and you shake to keep the pressure up.
  • Walls are found with Google's street-level positioning and the phone's own wall detection, so art stays on the same bricks.
  • Layering: go over what's there. Every wall keeps its history.
  • A map of every painted wall near you.

geo# is digital only. Please don't paint real walls.
